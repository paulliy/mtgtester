# Card authoring pilot

Date: 2026-10-31. Plan: [faster card authoring](../plans/card-authoring-and-fail-loud.md), phase 6.
ADR: [0033](../decisions/0033-forge-informed-card-authoring.md). Draw:
[card-authoring-pilot-draw.json](card-authoring-pilot-draw.json). Baseline:
[card authoring baseline](card-authoring-baseline-2026-10-28.md). Mini-pilot:
[card authoring mini-pilot](card-authoring-mini-pilot-2026-10-29.md).

Twenty cards new to the project, drawn by `bun run pilot:draw` and split 10 and 10 by a seeded hash,
were modeled to the completion standard on the main thread between 2026-10-30 17:09 and
2026-10-31 03:39 UTC, alternating arms:

- **New workflow** (`pilot-new`): `card:new` scaffolds the override and test file, and with
  `--primitive <op>` a new op in its five places with a contract test; JSON scenarios run by the
  scenario runner; and `card:finish` does snapshots, re-seal, lab registration and evidence
  binding.
- **Current workflow** (`pilot-current`): an override written by hand, Vitest behavior tests, a
  hand-written Card Test Lab batch with its own driver test and viewer spec, snapshots regenerated by
  `scripts/snapshot-override-defs.ts`, and the manifest entry typed by hand.

Both arms end at the same gate: manifest references, mutation (`mutate-cards`) with every mutant
killed or recorded as equivalent, viewer flows in the real Card Test Lab, `snapshots:check` and the
clean-tree card gate. Each card is one commit group: engine change (if any), the card, and the
`chore(benchmarks)` re-seal.

**The result is indicative only.** Ten cards per arm, one agent working both arms, the agent wrote the
new-workflow commands, the cards differ in shape, and time spent fixing the tools is counted in the
card that found the problem. The plan said so in advance (phase 6 step 1).

### Why the new workflow was not faster here

1. **Tool fixes were charged to new-workflow cards.** Face-Breaker (33.6 min) added the runner's
   `continue` options and `choiceAfterScript` and fixed `card:new`'s reason matching; Mithril Coat
   added `firstDecisionOf`; Takenuma added support-card loyalty. The current arm's lab
   infrastructure was built once, on Jet Medallion (27.6 min), and reused.
2. **The new arm writes more test.** Scenario JSON plus its test file came to a median of 482 test
   and lab lines per card against 257 for the control's Vitest tests and hand-written lab batch,
   because each scenario is also a viewer-ready lab scenario and each clause gets a negative case.
3. **The new arm drew harder cards.** Its cards needed a median of 35.5 engine lines outside
   `cards/` against the control's 8: seven of its ten changed the rules engine (Face-Breaker's lines
   are the runner), five of the control's did. The split was random; with ten cards a side, the
   draw dominates.
4. **The scaffold did not cover the hard parts.** The slowest new-workflow cards (Ragavan 41.0,
   Face-Breaker 33.6, Chalice 32.7) spent their time on engine features or fixes (dash and impulse
   exile, runner features, kick counts) written by hand, as they would be in the current workflow.
   `card:new` has no scaffold for rule statics, alternative costs or replacement fields. The
   slowest control cards spent theirs on the lab batch and an ESLint detour (Jet Medallion 27.6)
   and a parser fix (The Great Henge 25.4).
5. **The mechanical tail was already cheap.** `card:finish` saved the snapshot, re-seal and
   registration steps, but by the time the control arm had its lab batch those were a few minutes
   by hand.

Where the new workflow did better is correctness. Its scenario files are the lab scenarios, so a
card cannot pass its tests and fail in the lab for a reason the tests never exercised. Bolas's
Citadel, in the current workflow, did exactly that (finding 8).

One follow-up touched a new-workflow card after it landed: d1fa581 corrected the library order in
Sylvan Library's `payLifeOrTopDrawnCards` contract test, which had rotated the library by hand. It
is a test fix, not a `fix(...)` commit, and the card's behavior did not change.

## Findings

1. **Scenario runner gaps, fixed.** No way to play on through combat or stop at a choice
   (Face-Breaker): `continue` options and `choiceAfterScript` (ed07e77). No "first decision of a
   seat" helper (Mithril Coat): `firstDecisionOf` (4a261f0). Support cards could not carry loyalty
   (Takenuma, c6c514e). Identical option descriptions are one choice to the runner, so scripts
   name the prompt instead.
2. **`card:new` scaffold gaps, open.** It scaffolds triggered, activated and spell abilities and
   new ops, but not rule statics (Academy Manufactor), alternative costs (Ragavan's dash) or
   replacement fields (Mox Diamond, Everflowing Chalice). Those were written by hand in both arms.
3. **`card:new` reason matching, fixed.** It matched a reason to its line only in the line's own form
   (836733d).
4. **Mutation operator gaps, fixed with their cards.** No operators for an optional additional cost
   or its repeatable flag (Chalice), a mana ability's spend restriction (Courtyard), a trigger's
   aggregate and once-each-turn flags (Morbid Opportunist), dash (Ragavan), a library-top
   permission's life cost (Citadel), or `exileLibraryTop`'s free casts and its "each player" scope
   (Etali). Mutation counts only assertion failures as kills, so a test must assert before anything
   that can throw; several tests were reordered for it.
5. **Engine bugs found by modeling, fixed.** Entering permanents did not read the kick counts their
   spell paid (Chalice, 373908d). Restricted mana never bound its land's chosen creature type, which
   also fixed Cavern of Souls (Courtyard, 00b424d).
6. **Viewer bugs found by viewer flows, fixed.** The target picker did not open for a trigger already
   pending when the view mounted (Bowmasters, a5ee5f9). A card you may cast from an opponent's
   graveyard, exile or library top was not clickable (Ragavan, e6f338f).
7. **The current-workflow lab grows card by card.** The hand-written driver needed a mode filter,
   stack-object names, a no-block default and a blockers record for Untimely Malfunction, and a
   library top that is not drawn first for Bolas's Citadel. The runner already had their
   equivalents.
8. **A wrongly claimed line silently unmodels a card.** An override that claims a line the
   interpreter already parses leaves the card unmodeled. Bolas's Citadel's Vitest tests passed
   anyway, because they never ask whether the definition is modeled, and only its lab scenarios
   failed. Etali's first draft claimed its line with the card's name where the interpreter writes
   `~`; a check of the interpreted definition caught it before the tests ran.
   `card:new` prints exactly the unparsed lines, so the new arm cannot make this mistake.
9. **Ten-of-ten sacrifice asks nine times, open.** "Sacrifice ten nonland permanents" asks for each
   pick even when exactly ten candidates exist (the last is automatic). Changing it changes every
   multi-sacrifice decision stream, so it is left for its own change.
10. **A borderline rule static.** Academy Manufactor's `createClueFoodAndTreasure` is named for the
    effect it replaces, not the card, and no other printed card uses it today. It is a rule static,
    not an op, so the target holds, but it is the kind of one-card IR the ADR discourages.

## Typed results and duplicate families

Phase 6 builds typed results (ADR 0033 decision 3) when the first new-workflow card needs "its
controller", "those cards" or "that much" after another instruction. None did: Ragavan's "you may
cast that card" is the existing impulse-exile op. In the control arm, Etali's "cast any number of
spells from among those cards" went into `exileLibraryTop`'s existing free-cast flag, the kind of
in-op special case the ADR keeps until results exist. Results stay unbuilt; Etali is the first card
to move when they are.

No pilot card touched either measured duplicate family (the additional-trigger statics and the
damage multipliers), so neither was merged. `castFromZone` and `playFromZone` for the library top
overlap; Bolas's Citadel uses both, and merging them is left for its own change.

## Pre-merge code review

Before merge, reviewers each read one area of the branch: engine, table server, match and agent client,
web viewer, scripts, and the pilot cards' evidence. Of the 44 findings, 38 were fixed with a
regression test that fails without the fix, one turned out not to be a bug, and five stay open:

- **Guests filling a table's connections.** Guests can fill `maxConnections` and lock out the host or
  a seated player who reconnects. This needs many addresses, since the per-address limit holds; a
  reserved slot for the host and seats is the fix if it shows up in practice.
- **Large replay frames.** A `replayUnlocked` frame over 1 MiB would close the socket under the
  backpressure limit. No measured game comes near that size.
- **Pin store ordering.** It sorts with `localeCompare`; changing the comparison would reorder the
  committed pin store with no observed churn to fix.
- **`card-gate` argument length on Windows.** It passes its files to Vitest on one command line,
  14.6 KB of Windows' 32 KB; it will need a file list before the gate list doubles.
- **The skip lint.** It does not see `ctx.skip()`.

Separately, the Bun 1.3.14 `server.stop()` hang is worked around with a bounded wait, which a Bun
upgrade past the fix would let go.

The pilot evidence findings strengthened tests rather than definitions:

- Untimely Malfunction's target count is now tested on the real card.
- Gamble's seeded discard is shown to vary across seeds.
- Everflowing Chalice, Professional Face-Breaker, Takenuma, Orcish Bowmasters and Ragavan gained
  positive controls and missed cases.

Each new check fails against a deliberately broken definition.

Codex reviewed the ready PR on each push and raised twelve findings, all fixed with a
regression test that fails without the fix:

- Bolas's Citadel's life cast now covers each spell face: a split half, an adventure or omen, or a
  modal double-faced back face, each for its own mana value. Our review had left this one open; it
  is counted among the 38 above. A modal double-faced card's land face can also be played from the
  top, and a card with no mana cost is cast for 0 life.
- A resolving copy of a permanent spell now enters with the spell's kicker counts, dash, and Mox
  Diamond's discard replacement. A permanent entering as a copy of Mox Diamond (Sculpting Steel)
  and a token copy of it (Saheeli, Radiant Creator) get the replacement too.
- Academy Manufactor's three tokens are the predefined ones, even in place of a token copy that is
  a Clue, and they enter tapped when the token they replace would. A rider on the copies, such as
  Saheeli's end-step sacrifice, applies to all three.
- A new table's host token survives a storage write that throws.

## Recommendations

1. Keep the new workflow for cards whose clauses compose existing primitives and for every new op
   (`--primitive` filled the five per-op places for all three new ops, leaving the executor and
   any engine support, such as amass's continuous subtypes, to write), and extend `card:new` to
   scaffold rule statics, alternative costs and replacement fields, the hand-written parts that
   dominated the slow cards.
2. Make the current workflow's tests fail when a definition is unmodeled (finding 8), or retire the
   current workflow, since the runner covers what its hand-written labs needed.
3. Re-measure on the next batch with the tool fixes already in place; this pilot paid for them.
4. Count follow-up fixes at the 30-day mark (2026-11-30) and append them here.
