Whenever this creature attacks, put a +1/+1 counter on target creature (no controller restriction). This trigger is mandatory (no "you may").
Then each opponent is dealt damage equal to the number of permanents that meet all of the following, counted by you: are other than this creature; are controlled by you; are creatures; and have any counter, or are enchanted by an Aura you control, or are equipped.
The count is made after the counter is placed, so the targeted creature counts if it is a permanent you control that meets these conditions.
