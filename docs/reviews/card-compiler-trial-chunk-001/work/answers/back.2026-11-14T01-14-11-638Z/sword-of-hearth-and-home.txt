Whenever equipped creature deals combat damage to a player, you may choose up to one target creature you own. Blink each chosen creature (exile it, then return it to the battlefield under the control of this Equipment's controller). Then search a library for a basic land card and put it onto the battlefield (untapped, no count stated). Then shuffle. The trigger is mandatory; the "up to one" choice of target is the only optional part. No equip cost is shown.
