`ca22469` (seed `cloud-full-game-2026-10-05`, Constellation pod, built-in bots, full Scryfall

The trial also asked for a **Copy game report** button. Single-player games now have one (in the
action-log header during play, and in the header after the game). It copies a readable summary
and the full JSON from `POST /api/game-report`, which the server builds from the player's seat:
build commit and branch, game id, seed, controllers and pace, position, the player's pending
choice and last submitted action, the last 40 lines of the player's log, and decklists in engine
order. Opponents' decklists, imported URLs and pasted text are withheld until the game is over;
halts report their kind and turn only. Covered by `packages/server/src/match/report.test.ts`,
`packages/server/src/ws/game-report.test.ts`, `apps/web/src/lib/gameReport.test.ts` and the
seeded `apps/web/e2e/player-game.spec.ts`.
