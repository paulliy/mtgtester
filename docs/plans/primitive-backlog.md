# Primitive backlog: compiler refusals ranked by deck slots

Generated by `bun run primitive-backlog`. Do not edit by hand; edit `packages/server/fixtures/primitive-backlog-groups.json` and regenerate.

**Sample.** 221 cards the card compiler refused as inexpressible, from 2 trials (`docs/reviews/card-compiler-trial-01`, `docs/reviews/card-compiler-trial-02`). Each trial drew its cards from the pool of EDHREC cards blocked by one rules line, so this is a sample of the gaps, not a census. 220 are still blocked; 1 have since been modeled or compiled and leave the ranking.

**How the rank works.** A feature's score is the sum of its cards' EDHREC deck counts as a share of all 840,730,733 nonbasic deck slots. A card that needs k features counts 1/k toward each (6 cards do now; a need with no group of its own is listed on the card as `alsoNeeds`), so a card that also needs another missing feature does not look free. Slot share, not card count, separates a staple from a long-tail card. "XMage spec" counts cards XMage implements with shared ability classes only, where its code can serve as a specification. Machine-readable queue: `packages/server/fixtures/primitive-backlog.json`.
